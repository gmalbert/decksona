INSTALL ON YOUR HOME NETWORK

Bring Decksona
to your home screen.

  1. 01
    Copy the web/ files

    Place the contents of the project’s web/ folder in Home Assistant’s www/decksona/.

  2. 02
    Open it on your phone

    On home Wi-Fi, visit http://HOME_ASSISTANT_IP:8123/local/decksona/.

  3. 03
    Add to your home screen

    Use Safari’s Share menu on iPhone, or Chrome’s menu on Android, then connect your Music Assistant server.

Web app files & full setup guide on GitHub ↗

For home Wi-Fi: this setup uses local HTTP. Its access token is saved in the phone’s browser and travels over your local network without TLS. Don’t expose it to the internet or use it on public Wi-Fi.

05

UP AND PLAYING

Three steps to
your next favorite.

Decksona uses your Music Assistant server and its connected players to control playback.

01

Have Music Assistant ready.

Decksona connects to Music Assistant over your network. It needs a direct server address, such as http://your-server:8095.

About Music Assistant ↗
02

Create a long-lived token.

In Music Assistant, open Settings → Profile to create an access token for the connection.

03

Connect. Choose. Play.

Enter your server address and token in Decksona, select an available Music Assistant output, and search your library.

✳Using Decksona on your desktop? The Music Assistant Desktop Companion can provide a Sendspin output. Select that output in Decksona to play through your computer’s speakers.See the companion ↗
